I work at Anthropic as a Member of Technical Staff, based in NYC. Before that, I was a quant researcher at G-Research and then Tower Research Capital.

I hold a PhD in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University, advised by Mert Pilanci, and have collaborated across research areas such as large-scale optimization, reinforcement learning and imitation learning, with applications to safety-critical systems and robotics. I also served as a reviewer for NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR.
